**莲华上佛**（Padumuttara Buddha），二十四佛之一，在那罗陀佛之后出世。城名汉萨瓦提（Haṃsavatī），父为阿难陀剎帝利，母名善生；上首弟子为提婆罗与善生，侍者名善意，菩提树为娑罗罗树。佛陀座下众多大弟子的「第一」之愿，多发于此佛之世。

## 目录

## 出世

《佛种姓经》记："*Nāradassa aparena, sambuddho dvipaduttamo*"（那罗陀之后，有正觉者、两足中的最上者）、"*Padumuttaro nāma jino, akkhobho sāgarūpamo*"（胜者名莲华上，不可动摇如大海）。{{para|id=145-1699|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1699|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1700|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1700|style=reference}}

"*Maṇḍakappova so āsi, yamhi buddho ajāyatha*"（佛出世的那一劫是庄严劫）、"*Ussannakusalā janatā, tamhi kappe ajāyatha*"（那一劫中出生的人们善根深厚）。{{para|id=145-1702|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1702|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1703|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1703|style=reference}}

"*Padumuttarassa bhagavato, paṭhame dhammadesane*"（莲华上世尊初次说法时）、"*Koṭisatasahassānaṃ, dhammābhisamayo ahu*"（十万俱胝〔众生〕得法现观）。{{para|id=145-1705|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1705|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1706|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1706|style=reference}}

## 城邑与父母

"*Nagaraṃ haṃsavatī nāma, ānando nāma khattiyo*"（城名汉萨瓦提，剎帝利名阿难陀）、"*Sujātā nāma janikā, padumuttarassa satthuno*"（莲华上导师的生母名善生）。{{para|id=145-1752|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1752|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1753|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1753|style=reference}}

"*Dasavassasahassāni, agāraṃ ajjha so vasi*"（他在家住了一万年）、"*Naravāhano yaso vasavattī, tayo pāsādamuttamā*"（人乘、名称、自在——三座上妙宫殿）；"*Ticattārīsasahassāni, nāriyo samalaṅkatā*"（四万三千名盛装女子）、"*Vasudattā nāma nārī, uttamo nāma atrajo*"（其妻名婆苏达多，其子名郁多摩）。{{para|id=145-1755|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1755|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1756|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1756|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1758|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1758|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1759|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1759|style=reference}}

## 出家与转法轮

"*Nimitte caturo disvā, pāsādenābhinikkhami*"（见四种相后乘宫殿而出家）、"*Sattāhaṃ padhānacāraṃ, acarī purisuttamo*"（最上之人精勤修行七日）。{{para|id=145-1761|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1761|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1762|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1762|style=reference}}

"*Brahmunā yācito santo, padumuttaro vināyako*"（受梵天劝请，导师莲华上）、"*Vatti cakkaṃ mahāvīro, mithiluyyānamuttame*"（大雄在最上的弥提罗园中转动法轮）。{{para|id=145-1764|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1764|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1765|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1765|style=reference}}

## 弟子与菩提树

"*Devalo ca sujāto ca, ahesuṃ aggasāvakā*"（提婆罗与善生是上首弟子）、"*Sumano nāmupaṭṭhāko, padumuttarassa mahesino*"（莲华上大仙的侍者名善意）。{{para|id=145-1767|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1767|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1768|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1768|style=reference}}

"*Amitā ca asamā ca, ahesuṃ aggasāvikā*"（阿弥多与阿萨摩是上首弟子尼）、"*Bodhi tassa bhagavato, salaloti pavuccati*"（那位世尊的菩提树称为娑罗罗）。{{para|id=145-1770|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1770|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1771|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1771|style=reference}}

"*Vitiṇṇo ceva tisso ca, ahesuṃ aggupaṭṭhakā*"（毗底那与帝须是上首优婆塞）、"*Haṭṭhā ceva vicittā ca, ahesuṃ aggupaṭṭhikā*"（诃塔与毗质多是上首优婆夷）。{{para|id=145-1773|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1773|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1774|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1774|style=reference}}

## 身量、寿量与般涅槃

"*Aṭṭhapaṇṇāsaratanaṃ, accuggato mahāmuni*"（大牟尼身高五十八肘）；"*Na tassāvaraṇaṃ atthi, samantā dvādasayojane*"（周围十二由旬内无有障蔽）。{{para|id=145-1776|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1776|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1780|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1780|style=reference}}

"*Vassasatasahassāni, āyu vijjati tāvade*"（那时的寿量是十万年）；"*Jalitvā aggikkhandhova nibbuto so sasāvako*"（他与弟子们如火聚燃尽而般涅槃）、"*Padumuttaro jino buddho, nandārāmamhi nibbuto*"（胜者莲华上佛在难陀园中般涅槃）。{{para|id=145-1782|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1782|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1786|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1786|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-1788|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 1788|style=reference}}

## 「第一」之愿的发起处

释迦佛座下诸大弟子的名号之愿多发于此佛之世。例如优波离："*padumuttarabuddhakāle kiresa haṃsavatiyaṃ kulaghare nibbatto ekadivasaṃ satthu dhammakathaṃ suṇanto satthāraṃ ekaṃ bhikkhuṃ vinayadharānaṃ aggaṭṭhāne ṭhapentaṃ disvā adhikārakammaṃ katvā taṃ ṭhānantaraṃ patthesi*"（据说他在莲华上佛时代生于汉萨瓦提的良家；某日听导师说法，见导师把一位比丘立于持律第一之位，便作殊胜之业而发愿求此位）。{{para|id=99-993|title=manorathapūraṇī 993|style=reference}}

阿那律亦然，佛陀为其记别："*ambho purisa, anāgate kappasatasahassapariyosāne gotamo nāma buddho uppajjissati, tassa sāsane tvaṃ dibbacakkhukānaṃ aggo anuruddho nāma bhavissasī*"（善男子，未来十万劫之末将有佛名乔达摩出世，在他的教法中，你将成为天眼第一，名叫阿那律）。{{para|id=99-662|title=manorathapūraṇī 662|style=reference}}
