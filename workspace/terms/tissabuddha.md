**帝沙佛**（Tissa Buddha），二十四佛之一，在悉达多佛之后出世。城名差摩迦（Khemaka），父为闍那删陀剎帝利，母名波头摩；上首弟子为梵天提婆与优陀耶，侍者名三鸯伽，菩提树为阿萨那树。事迹见《佛种姓经·帝沙佛种姓》。同名者见 [[tissa]] 消歧页。

## 目录

## 出世

《佛种姓经》记："*Siddhatthassa aparena, asamo appaṭipuggalo*"（悉达多之后，有无等、无与伦比者）、"*Anantatejo amitayaso, tisso lokagganāyako*"（无边威光、无量名称的帝沙，世间最上导师）。{{para|id=145-2306|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2306|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2307|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2307|style=reference}}

"*Tamandhakāraṃ vidhamitvā, obhāsetvā sadevakaṃ*"（破除黑暗，照耀含天世间）、"*Anukampako mahāvīro, loke uppajji cakkhumā*"（悲愍的大雄、具眼者出现于世）。{{para|id=145-2309|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2309|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2310|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2310|style=reference}}

## 三次现观

"*Tassāpi atulā iddhi, atulaṃ sīlaṃ samādhi ca*"（他的神力无比，戒与定也无比）、"*Sabbattha pāramiṃ gantvā, dhammacakkaṃ pavattayi*"（一切处皆达究竟，转动法轮）。{{para|id=145-2312|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2312|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2313|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2313|style=reference}}

"*So buddho dasasahassimhi, viññāpesi giraṃ suciṃ*"（那位佛在一万〔世界〕中宣示清净之音）、"*Koṭisatāni abhisamiṃsu, paṭhame dhammadesane*"（初次说法时百俱胝〔众生〕得现观）；"*Dutiyo navutikoṭīnaṃ, tatiyo saṭṭhikoṭiyo*"（第二次九十俱胝，第三次六十俱胝）、"*Bandhanāto pamocesi, satte naramarū tadā*"（那时使人天众生从系缚中解脱）。{{para|id=145-2315|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2315|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2316|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2316|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2318|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2318|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2319|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2319|style=reference}}

## 城邑与父母

"*Khemakaṃ nāma nagaraṃ, janasandho nāma khattiyo*"（城名差摩迦，剎帝利名闍那删陀）、"*Padumā nāma janikā, tissassa ca mahesino*"（帝沙大仙的生母名波头摩）。{{para|id=145-2350|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2350|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2351|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2351|style=reference}}

"*Guhāsela nārisaya nisabhā, tayo pāsādamuttamā*"（窟舍、那利舍耶、尼萨婆——三座上妙宫殿）；"*Samatiṃsasahassāni, nāriyo samalaṅkatā*"（三万名盛装女子）、"*Subhaddānāmikā nārī, ānando nāma atrajo*"（其妻名须跋陀，其子名阿难）。{{para|id=145-2354|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2354|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2356|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2356|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2357|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2357|style=reference}}

## 出家与转法轮

"*Nimitte caturo disvā, assayānena nikkhami*"（见四种相后乘马出家）、"*Anūnaaṭṭhamāsāni, padhānaṃ padahī jino*"（胜者精勤修行足足八个月）。{{para|id=145-2359|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2359|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2360|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2360|style=reference}}

"*Brahmunā yācito santo, tisso lokagganāyako*"（受梵天劝请，世间最上导师帝沙）、"*Vatti cakkaṃ mahāvīro, yasavatiyamuttame*"（大雄在最上的耶输婆提转动法轮）。{{para|id=145-2362|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2362|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2363|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2363|style=reference}}

## 弟子与菩提树

"*Brahmadevo udayo ca, ahesuṃ aggasāvakā*"（梵天提婆与优陀耶是上首弟子）、"*Samaṅgo nāmupaṭṭhāko, tissassa ca mahesino*"（帝沙大仙的侍者名三鸯伽）。{{para|id=145-2365|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2365|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2366|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2366|style=reference}}

"*Phussā ceva sudattā ca, ahesuṃ aggasāvikā*"（弗沙与善施是上首弟子尼）、"*Bodhi tassa bhagavato, asanoti pavuccati*"（那位世尊的菩提树称为阿萨那）。{{para|id=145-2368|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2368|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2369|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2369|style=reference}}

"*Sambalo ca sirimā ceva, ahesuṃ aggupaṭṭhakā*"（三婆罗与尸利摩是上首优婆塞）、"*Kisāgotamī upasenā, ahesuṃ aggupaṭṭhikā*"（翅舍乔答弥与优波先那是上首优婆夷）。{{para|id=145-2371|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2371|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2372|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2372|style=reference}}

## 菩萨受记

《佛种姓经》中我们的菩萨在此佛处受记："*Tamahaṃ pupphaṃ gahetvā, matthake dhārayiṃ jinaṃ*"（我取来那花，擎于胜者头顶之上）、"*Sopi maṃ buddho byākāsi, janamajjhe nisīdiya*"（那位佛也在众中坐着为我授记）；"*Uttariṃ vatamadhiṭṭhāsiṃ, dasapāramipūriyā*"（我更受持誓行，为圆满十波罗蜜）。{{para|id=145-2340|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2340|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2342|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2342|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2348|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2348|style=reference}}
