**乔达摩佛**（Gotamabuddha），即释迦牟尼、当今佛陀。「乔达摩」是他的**姓氏**而非名字，故外道与婆罗门常以「乔达摩尊者」（bho gotama）相称。《佛种姓经·乔达摩佛种姓》以第一人称记其城邑、父母、出家、成道、说法与教团。同姓者及同名长老见 [[gotama]] 消歧页。

## 目录

## 自述：现在的正觉者

《佛种姓经》以佛陀自述开篇："*Ahametarahi sambuddho, gotamo sakyavaḍḍhano*"（我是现在的正觉者，乔达摩，释迦族的增荣者）、"*Padhānaṃ padahitvāna, patto sambodhimuttamaṃ*"（精勤修行，证得无上正觉）。{{para|id=145-3089|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3089|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3090|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3090|style=reference}}

"*Bārāṇasiyaṃ isipatane, cakkaṃ pavattitaṃ mayā*"（我在波罗奈仙人堕处转法轮）、"*Ahaṃ gotamasambuddho, saraṇaṃ sabbapāṇinaṃ*"（我，乔达摩正觉者，是一切众生的归依）。{{para|id=145-3137|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3137|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3138|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3138|style=reference}}

## 城邑与父母

"*Nagaraṃ kapilavatthu me, rājā suddhodano pitā*"（我的城是迦毗罗卫，父亲是净饭王）、"*Mayhaṃ janettikā mātā, māyādevīti vuccati*"（我的生母名为摩耶夫人）。{{para|id=145-3125|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3125|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3126|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3126|style=reference}}

## 在家与出家

"*Ekūnatiṃsavassāni, agāraṃ ajjhahaṃ vasiṃ*"（我在家住了二十九年）、"*Rammo surammo subhako, tayo pāsādamuttamā*"（喜、极喜、净——是三座上妙宫殿）。{{para|id=145-3128|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3128|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3129|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3129|style=reference}}

"*Cattārīsasahassāni, nāriyo samalaṅkatā*"（四万名盛装女子）、"*Bhaddakañcanā nāma nārī, rāhulo nāma atrajo*"（贤金〔耶输陀罗〕是我的妻，罗睺罗是我的儿子）。{{para|id=145-3131|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3131|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3132|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3132|style=reference}}

"*Nimitte caturo disvā, assayānena nikkhamiṃ*"（见四种相后，我乘马出家）、"*Chabbassaṃ padhānacāraṃ, acariṃ dukkaraṃ ahaṃ*"（我行了六年难行的精勤）。{{para|id=145-3134|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3134|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3135|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3135|style=reference}}

"*Ahaṃ assatthamūlamhi, patto sambodhimuttamaṃ*"（我在毕钵罗树下证得无上正觉）、"*Byāmappabhā sadā mayhaṃ, soḷasahatthamuggatā*"（我常有一寻的光明，高十六肘）。{{para|id=145-3147|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3147|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3148|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3148|style=reference}}

## 三次法的现观

"*Brahmunā yācito santo, dhammacakkaṃ pavattayiṃ*"（我受梵天劝请而转法轮）、"*Aṭṭhārasannaṃ koṭīnaṃ, paṭhamābhisamayo ahu*"（十八俱胝〔众生〕有了第一次现观）。{{para|id=145-3092|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3092|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3093|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3093|style=reference}}

"*Tato parañca desente, naradevasamāgame*"（此后在人天集会中说法时）、"*Gaṇanāya na vattabbo, dutiyābhisamayo ahu*"（第二次现观多得无法计数）；"*Idhevāhaṃ etarahi, ovadiṃ mama atrajaṃ*"（如今我就在此教诫我的儿子）、"*Gaṇanāya na vattabbo, tatiyābhisamayo ahu*"（第三次现观也多得无法计数）。{{para|id=145-3095|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3095|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3096|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3096|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3098|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3098|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3099|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3099|style=reference}}

## 弟子

"*Kolito upatisso ca, dve bhikkhū aggasāvakā*"（拘律陀与优波底沙，是两位上首弟子比丘）、"*Ānando nāmupaṭṭhāko, santikāvacaro mama*"（阿难是侍者，常在我身边）、"*Khemā uppalavaṇṇā ca, bhikkhunī aggasāvikā*"（差摩与莲华色，是上首弟子比丘尼）。{{para|id=145-3140|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3140|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3141|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3141|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3142|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3142|style=reference}}

"*Citto hatthāḷavako ca, aggupaṭṭhākupāsakā*"（质多与阿罗婆的手长者，是上首优婆塞）、"*Nandamātā ca uttarā, aggupaṭṭhikupāsikā*"（难陀母与郁多罗，是上首优婆夷）。{{para|id=145-3144|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3144|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3145|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3145|style=reference}}

僧团集会："*Ekosi sannipāto me, sāvakānaṃ mahesinaṃ*"（我的大仙弟子只有一次集会）、"*Aḍḍhateḷasasatānaṃ, bhikkhūnāsi samāgamo*"（是一千二百五十位比丘的聚会）。{{para|id=145-3101|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3101|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3102|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3102|style=reference}}

## 教法与寿量

"*Vitthārikaṃ bāhujaññaṃ, iddhaṃ phītaṃ suphullitaṃ*"（广布、为众人所知、兴盛、繁荣、盛开）、"*Idha mayhaṃ sakyamunino, sāsanaṃ suvisodhitaṃ*"（我释迦牟尼的教法，在此得到善净化）。{{para|id=145-3113|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3113|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3114|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3114|style=reference}}

"*Appaṃ vassasataṃ āyu, idānetarahi vijjati*"（如今的寿量不过百年）、"*Tāvatā tiṭṭhamānohaṃ, tāremi janataṃ bahuṃ*"（我住世那么久，度脱许多人）。{{para|id=145-3150|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3150|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-3151|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 3151|style=reference}}

## 「乔达摩」是姓氏

《善妙悦意（长部义注）》释外道称呼佛陀的套语："*Samaṇoti bhagavā samitapāpatāya laddhavohāro*"（「沙门」是世尊因息灭恶而得的称呼），"*Gotamoti gottavasena*"（「乔达摩」是依姓氏而言）。{{para|id=103-424|title=sumaṅgalavilāsinī 424|style=reference}}

同书释「沙门乔达摩」这一传闻套语："*samaṇo khalu bho gotamo*"（诸位，据说沙门乔达摩……）；"*Bhagavā ca anuttarena ariyamaggena samitapāpo. Tenassa yathābhūtaguṇādhigatametaṃ nāmaṃ, yadidaṃ samaṇoti*"（世尊以无上圣道息灭了恶，所以「沙门」这个名称是他依如实之德而得的）；"*Gotamoti bhagavantaṃ gottavasena parikitteti*"（「乔达摩」是依姓氏称呼世尊）。{{para|id=103-1049|title=sumaṅgalavilāsinī 1049|style=reference}}

《显扬心义疏》进一步解释「姓」之义："*Gottavasenāti ettha gaṃ tāyatīti gottaṃ*"（「依姓氏」，此中「护持所称」即是姓）；"*So pana aññakulaparamparāya asādhāraṇaṃ tassa kulassa ādipurisasamudāgataṃ taṃkulapariyāpannasādhāraṇaṃ sāmaññarūpanti daṭṭhabbaṃ*"（应知姓是不与他族共通、源自该族始祖、而为该族成员所共有的通名）。{{para|id=204-856|title=sāratthadīpanī-ṭīkā 856|style=reference}}

并说明这两个称呼各自的分量："*samaṇoti iminā sarikkhakajanehi bhagavato bahumatabhāvo dassito samitapāpatākittanato, gotamoti iminā lokiyajanehi uḷārakulasambhūtatādīpanato*"（以「沙门」显示同类之人因称扬其息恶而对世尊的推重，以「乔达摩」显示世人所知其出身高贵之族）。{{para|id=204-856|title=sāratthadīpanī-ṭīkā 856|style=reference}}
