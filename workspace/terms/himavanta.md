**雪山（Himavanta，亦作 Himavā）**即喜马拉雅山，巴利文献中的「山王」（pabbatarāja）。它是恒河等大河的发源地，是独觉、仙人与苦行者的居处，也是本生故事中菩萨作仙人时的常住之地。经中并常以雪山之巨对比芥子之微，用以显示善恶果报或苦之多寡的悬殊。

## 名称与语源

《名义灯复注（Abhidhānappadīpikā-ṭīkā）》在《林等品释（6. Araññādivaggavaṇṇanā）》中给出二解："*Himappacuratāya himavā, himaṃ vā vamatīti himavā, kvi, rājādipakkhepattā sissā. Himayutto acalo himācalo*"（因多雪故名雪山；或谓「吐雪」故名雪山……与雪相连的不动〔山〕即是雪山）。{{para|id=9-1676|title=abhidhānappadīpikāṭīkā 1676|style=reference}}

《声律论（Saddanīti，词根鬘品）》在《以 m 结尾的词根》中引本生义注的两说："*'himavāti himapātasamaye himayuttoti himavā. Gimhakāle himaṃ vamatīti himavā' ti vuttaṃ*"（「雪山」，谓降雪时与雪相连故名雪山；夏时吐雪故名雪山）。{{para|id=16-1167|title=saddanītippakaraṇaṃ (dhātumālā) 1167|style=reference}}《譬喻义注（Apadāna-aṭṭhakathā）》在《佉陀罗林长老譬喻释（3-9. Khadiravaniyattheraapadānavaṇṇanā）》中另出一解："*satte hiṃsati sītena hanati matheti āloḷetīti himo, himo assa atthīti himavā*"（以寒害众生、以寒击恼扰乱，故名「雪」；有雪故名雪山）。{{para|id=107-2099|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 2099|style=reference}}该书在《第 141 颂》中简作："*himo assa atthīti himavā, tassa himavantassa avidūre samīpe*"（有雪故名雪山；在那雪山不远的近处）。{{para|id=107-1459|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 1459|style=reference}}

《波逸提等文法解（Pācityādiyojanā）》在《坏生种学处义释（1. Bhūtagāmasikkhāpada-atthayojanā）》中释此词的处格："*Himavanteti himauggiraṇe vane, himayutte vā*"（「于雪山」谓于吐雪之林，或于与雪相连〔之处〕）。{{para|id=202-82|title=pācityādiyojanā 82|style=reference}}

## 语法书中的词形

`Himavanta` 的主格单数有 `himavanto` 与 `himavā` 两形。《用法成就（Payogasiddhipāḷi）》在《第 153 条「雪山〔词尾〕或作 o」（153. Himavato vā o）》中立规则："*Himavato simhi ntussa o vā hoti, himavanto himavā*"（雪山一词在主格单数中，`nt` 或变为 `o`，故有 himavanto、himavā 二形）。{{para|id=19-539|title=payogasiddhipāḷi 539|style=reference}}《语源灯（Niruttidīpanīpāṭha）》在《德说等类聚（Guṇavādigaṇarāsi）》中列同一规则{{para|id=22-968|title=niruttidīpanīpāṭha 968|style=reference}}，并见其下文{{para|id=22-970|title=niruttidīpanīpāṭha 970|style=reference}}。《目犍连文法（Moggallānabyākaraṇa）》第二品{{para|id=13-1567|title=moggallānabyākaraṇaṃ 1567|style=reference}}与《目犍连注释疏（Moggallāna vuttivivaraṇapañcikā）》{{para|id=52-460|title=moggallāna vuttivivaraṇapañcikā. 460|style=reference}}中亦及之。

《声律论（词鬘品）》在《以 ā 结尾的阳性名词变化（6. Ākārantapulliṅganāmikapadamālā）》中辨其单复数："*Tatra 'himavanto' ti idaṃ yebhuyyenekavacanaṃ bhavati, katthaci bahuvacanampi, tenāha niruttipiṭake thero 'himavā tiṭṭhati, himavanto tiṭṭhantī' ti*"（其中「himavanto」多为单数，某些处亦作复数，故语源藏中长老说「雪山住立（单）、诸雪山住立（复）」）。{{para|id=15-1617|title=saddanītippakaraṇaṃ (padamālā) 1617|style=reference}}该书在《具抉择数词变化（13. Savinicchayasaṅkhyānāmanāmikapadamālā）》中亦及之。{{para|id=15-2860|title=saddanītippakaraṇaṃ (padamālā) 2860|style=reference}}

## 规模与地理

《清净道论大复注（Visuddhimagga-mahāṭīkā）》在《第 137 节》中释其高度："*Yojanānaṃ satānucco, himavā pañca pabbatoti himavā pabbato pañca yojanānaṃ satāni ucco ubbedho*"（「高五百由旬的雪山」，谓雪山高五百由旬）。{{para|id=66-831|title=visuddhimagga-mahāṭīkā 831|style=reference}}《疑惑度脱新注（Vimativinodanī-ṭīkā）》在《毗兰若品释（Verañjakaṇḍavaṇṇanā）》中所释相同。{{para|id=210-174|title=vimativinodanī-ṭīkā 174|style=reference}}

《佛种姓义注（Buddhavaṃsa-aṭṭhakathā）》在《第 27 章》中以其为高远之喻："*yathā yojanānaṃ satānucco himavā pañcapabbato sudūre ṭhitānampi uccabhāvena ca sommabhāvena ca atiramaṇīyo hutvā dissati, evaṃ bhagavāpi dissatī*"（如高五百由旬的雪山五峰，即使远立者亦见其高峻柔和、极为可爱，世尊亦如是可见）。{{para|id=108-3219|title=buddhavaṃsa-aṭṭhakathā 3219|style=reference}}

《显隐义复注（Līnatthappakāsanā）》在《三十二大人相释（Dvattiṃsamahāpurisalakkhaṇavaṇṇanā）》中，把雪山与须弥并列为轮围的主要部分。{{para|id=186-120|title=līnatthappakāsanā 120|style=reference}}

《吉祥悦意（Sumaṅgalavilāsinī）》在《婆罗门使者事释（Brāhmaṇadūtavatthuvaṇṇanā）》中记毗舍离城外的大林与雪山连成一片{{para|id=185-1132|title=līnatthappakāsanā 1132|style=reference}}（此语的疏解另见《善妙义复注》{{para|id=189-996|title=sādhuvilāsinī 996|style=reference}}）。《经集义注（Suttanipāta-aṭṭhakathā）》在《出家经释（1. Pabbajjāsuttavaṇṇanā）》{{para|id=125-1688|title=suttanipāta-aṭṭhakathā 1688|style=reference}}与《正遍行经释（13. Sammāparibbājanīyasuttavaṇṇanā）》{{para|id=125-1592|title=suttanipāta-aṭṭhakathā 1592|style=reference}}中，亦以雪山为释迦族建城之地的所在。

## 作为譬喻

以雪山对比芥子是经中的定型譬喻。《相应部（Saṃyuttanikāyapāḷi）》在《山经（9. Pabbatasuttaṃ）》中说："*seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, puriso himavato pabbatarājassa satta sāsapamattiyo pāsāṇasakkharā upanikkhipeyya*"（诸比库，譬如有人于雪山王旁置七粒芥子大的石砾）。{{para|id=168-655|title=saṃyuttanikāyapāḷi 655|style=reference}}该书在《第一山喻经（9. Paṭhamapabbatūpamasuttaṃ）》{{para|id=171-2709|title=saṃyuttanikāyapāḷi 2709|style=reference}}与《第二山喻经（10. Dutiyapabbatūpamasuttaṃ）》{{para|id=171-2711|title=saṃyuttanikāyapāḷi 2711|style=reference}}中重出此喻。

《中部（Majjhimanikāyapāḷi）》在《愚贤经（9. Bālapaṇḍitasuttaṃ）》中以同一对比问答："*'katamo nu kho mahantataro – yo cāyaṃ mayā paritto pāṇimatto pāsāṇo gahito, yo ca himavā pabbatarājā' ti?*"（「何者为大——我所取的这一掌大的小石，还是雪山王？」）；比库答曰雪山之于小石，不可以数计。{{para|id=166-737|title=majjhimanikāyapāḷi 737|style=reference}}

《相应部》在《猕猴经（7. Makkaṭasuttaṃ）》中，以雪山中猕猴出没的险处为喻。{{para|id=171-904|title=saṃyuttanikāyapāḷi 904|style=reference}}

《譬喻义注》在《第 191 颂》中以雪山喻佛："*Yathāpi selo himavāti himavā nāma selamayapabbato … Yathā so himavā nāgādīnaṃ ālayo, tathā tevijjāya ca chaḷabhiññāya ca iddhiyā ca pāramiṃ pariyosānaṃ gatā pattā tuvaṃ nissāya vasantī*"（「如石造的雪山」，雪山是石造之山……如雪山是龙等的居处，得三明、六通与神变之究竟者亦依你而住）。{{para|id=107-1743|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 1743|style=reference}}

## 仙人与独觉的居处

《譬喻义注》在《舍利弗长老譬喻释（3-1. Sāriputtattheraapadānavaṇṇanā）》中引其偈："*Himavantassa avidūre, lambako nāma pabbato*"（离雪山不远，有山名蓝婆迦）{{para|id=107-1443|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 1443|style=reference}}，并释："*tassa himavantassa avidūre samīpe, himālayapaṭibaddhavanehi attho*"（在那雪山不远的近处，义即与喜马拉雅相连的诸林）。{{para|id=107-1459|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 1459|style=reference}}《须菩提长老譬喻释（1. Subhūtittheraapadānavaṇṇanā）》中作："*himavantassāti himālayapabbatassa avidūre āsanne samīpe pabbatapāde manussānaṃ gamanāgamanasampanne sañcaraṇaṭṭhāneti attho*"（「雪山的」谓在喜马拉雅山不远的近处、山麓、人所往来通行之处）。{{para|id=107-2336|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 2336|style=reference}}

同类记载另见《宾头卢婆罗豆婆遮长老譬喻释（3-8. Piṇḍolabhāradvājattheraapadānavaṇṇanā）》："*Purato himavantassāti himālayapabbatato pubbadisābhāgeti attho*"（「雪山之前」谓在喜马拉雅山的东方）{{para|id=107-2083|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 2083|style=reference}}，以及《周利盘陀长老譬喻释（4. Cūḷapanthakattheraapadānavaṇṇanā）》{{para|id=107-2191|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 2191|style=reference}}、《独觉佛譬喻释（2. Paccekabuddhaapadānavaṇṇanā）》{{para|id=107-1256|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 1256|style=reference}}、《光明想长老譬喻释（5. Raṃsisaññakattheraapadānavaṇṇanā）》{{para|id=107-2730|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 2730|style=reference}}、《供栴檀长老譬喻释（3. Candanapūjanakattheraapadānavaṇṇanā）》{{para|id=107-2948|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 2948|style=reference}}。世尊亦曾入雪山："*sambuddho vivekakāmo ekībhāvaṃ icchanto himavantaṃ himālayapabbataṃ upāgamī*"（正觉者乐远离、欲独处，遂往雪山）{{para|id=107-1671|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 1671|style=reference}}，「*Ajjhogāhetvā himavantan ti himavantasamīpaṃ ogāhetvā pavisitvāti attho*"（「深入雪山」，义谓入于雪山近处）{{para|id=107-1672|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 1672|style=reference}}。

《经集义注》在《犀角经释（3. Khaggavisāṇasuttavaṇṇanā）》中亦以雪山为独觉的住处。{{para|id=125-254|title=suttanipāta-aṭṭhakathā 254|style=reference}}

## 大河的发源

《譬喻义注》在《第 191 颂》中记五大河出自雪山："*Gaṅgā, yamunā, sarabhū, mahī, aciravatīti imāsaṃ pañcannaṃ gaṅgānaṃ antare ayaṃ bhāgīrathī nāma paṭhamamahāgaṅgā himavantā pabhāvitā himavantato āgatā anotattadahato pabhavā*"（恒河、耶牟那、萨罗浮、摩企、阿致罗筏底——此五大河中，这条名为婆耆罗底的第一大恒河出自雪山、自雪山而来、源于无热恼池）。{{para|id=107-1698|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 1698|style=reference}}《佉陀罗林长老譬喻释》中释同一语："*tato himavantato paṭṭhāya pabhāvitā pavattā sandamānāti himavantapabhāvitā*"（自那雪山起而流出、流注，故名「出自雪山」）。{{para|id=107-2099|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 2099|style=reference}}
