**频毗娑罗**（Bimbisāra），摩揭陀国王，全称「摩揭陀王斯尼耶频毗娑罗」。佛陀成道后不久他在王舍城闻法证须陀洹，自陈少年时的五愿今已圆满，并把**竹林园**献给以佛为首的僧团——这是僧团所受的第一座园林。

## 目录

## 名号

《自说经义注》逐词解释其称号："*Dānādīhi catūhi saṅgahavatthūhi parisaṃ rañjetīti rājā*"（以布施等四摄事令众欢悦，故名「王」）；"*Magadhānaṃ issaroti māgadho*"（摩揭陀人之主，故名「摩揭陀〔王〕」）；"*Mahatiyā senāya samannāgatattā seniyagottattā vā seniyo*"（因具足大军，或因属斯尼耶姓，故名「斯尼耶」）。{{para|id=123-358|title=udāna-aṭṭhakathā 358|style=reference}}

"*Bimbi vuccati suvaṇṇaṃ, tasmā sārabimbivaṇṇatāya bimbisāro*"（「频毗」谓黄金，因其色如精金，故名「频毗娑罗」）；"*Keci pana 'nāmamevetaṃ tassa rañño'ti vadanti*"（也有人说这只是那位王的名字）。{{para|id=123-358|title=udāna-aṭṭhakathā 358|style=reference}}

## 少年五愿

《律藏·大品》记他闻法证果后自述："*Atha kho rājā māgadho seniyo bimbisāro diṭṭhadhammo pattadhammo viditadhammo pariyogāḷhadhammo tiṇṇavicikiccho vigatakathaṃkatho vesārajjappatto aparappaccayo satthusāsane bhagavantaṃ etadavoca*"（当时摩揭陀王斯尼耶频毗娑罗见法、得法、知法、深入于法，度疑离惑，得无所畏，于导师的教法中不依他缘，对世尊说）：{{para|id=215-283|title=vinayapitaka 283|style=reference}}

"*pubbe me, bhante, kumārassa sato pañca assāsakā ahesuṃ, te me etarahi samiddhā*"（大德，我年少时曾有五个愿望，如今都圆满了）：{{para|id=215-283|title=vinayapitaka 283|style=reference}}

"*'aho vata maṃ rajje abhisiñceyyun'ti, ayaṃ kho me, bhante, paṭhamo assāsako ahosi*"（「但愿我得受王位灌顶」——这是我的第一愿）；"*'Tassa ca me vijitaṃ arahaṃ sammāsambuddho okkameyyā'ti, ayaṃ kho me, bhante, dutiyo assāsako ahosi*"（「但愿阿罗汉、正等觉者莅临我的国土」——这是第二愿）；"*'Tañcāhaṃ bhagavantaṃ payirupāseyyan'ti, ayaṃ kho me, bhante, tatiyo assāsako ahosi*"（「但愿我得亲近那位世尊」——这是第三愿）；"*'So ca me bhagavā dhammaṃ deseyyā'ti, ayaṃ kho me, bhante, catuttho assāsako ahosi*"（「但愿那位世尊为我说法」——这是第四愿）。{{para|id=215-283|title=vinayapitaka 283|style=reference}}

他随即归依："*Esāhaṃ, bhante, bhagavantaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi, dhammañca, bhikkhusaṅghañca. Upāsakaṃ maṃ, bhagavā dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṃ saraṇaṃ gataṃ, adhivāsetu ca me, bhante, bhagavā, svātanāya bhattaṃ saddhiṃ bhikkhusaṅghenā*"（大德，我归依世尊、法与比丘僧团；愿世尊记我为优婆塞，从今日起尽形寿归依；并愿世尊接受我明日与比丘僧团的供食）。{{para|id=215-283|title=vinayapitaka 283|style=reference}}

## 佛陀入王舍城

"*Atha kho bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṃ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya rājagahaṃ pāvisi mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṃ bhikkhusahassena sabbeheva purāṇajaṭilehi*"（世尊于上午着衣持钵，与一千位全是旧日结发行者的大比丘僧团一同入王舍城）。{{para|id=215-284|title=vinayapitaka 284|style=reference}}

其时帝释化作童子在前引导而歌："*Danto dantehi saha purāṇajaṭilehi, vippamutto vippamuttehi*"（已调伏者与已调伏的旧结发行者同行，已解脱者与已解脱者同行）；众人问那童子是谁，帝释答："*Yo dhīro sabbadhi danto, suddho appaṭipuggalo*"（那位智者一切处已调伏，清净、无与伦比）、"*Arahaṃ sugato loke, tassāhaṃ paricārako*"（世间的阿罗汉、善逝——我是他的侍者）。{{para|id=215-285|title=vinayapitaka 285|style=reference}}{{para|id=215-299|title=vinayapitaka 299|style=reference}}{{para|id=215-300|title=vinayapitaka 300|style=reference}}{{para|id=215-301|title=vinayapitaka 301|style=reference}}

## 献竹林园

"*Atha kho bhagavā yena rañño māgadhassa seniyassa bimbisārassa nivesanaṃ tenupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā paññatte āsane nisīdi saddhiṃ bhikkhusaṅghena*"（世尊来到摩揭陀王斯尼耶频毗娑罗的宫中，与比丘僧团一同就座）；供食毕，王思忖："*kattha nu kho bhagavā vihareyya*"（世尊该住在哪里呢）。{{para|id=215-302|title=vinayapitaka 302|style=reference}}

他所悬的条件是："*Yaṃ assa gāmato neva avidūre na accāsanne, gamanāgamanasampannaṃ, atthikānaṃ atthikānaṃ manussānaṃ abhikkamanīyaṃ, divā appākiṇṇaṃ, rattiṃ appasaddaṃ appanigghosaṃ vijanavātaṃ, manussarāhasseyyakaṃ, paṭisallānasāruppan*"（既不太远又不太近于村落，往来方便，需要的人都能前往，白天不喧杂，夜里少声、少噪、无人往来之风，宜于人独处，适于静坐）。{{para|id=215-302|title=vinayapitaka 302|style=reference}}

"*idaṃ kho amhākaṃ veḷuvanaṃ uyyānaṃ … paṭisallānasāruppaṃ. Yaṃnūnāhaṃ veḷuvanaṃ uyyānaṃ buddhappamukhassa bhikkhusaṅghassa dadeyyan*"（我们这座竹林园……正适于静坐；我何不把竹林园献给以佛为首的比丘僧团呢）。{{para|id=215-302|title=vinayapitaka 302|style=reference}}

"*Atha kho rājā māgadho seniyo bimbisāro sovaṇṇamayaṃ bhiṅkāraṃ gahetvā bhagavato oṇojesi – 'etāhaṃ, bhante, veḷuvanaṃ uyyānaṃ buddhappamukhassa bhikkhusaṅghassa dammī'ti*"（于是摩揭陀王斯尼耶频毗娑罗取金瓶〔注水〕而奉献世尊：「大德，我把这座竹林园献给以佛为首的比丘僧团」）。{{para|id=215-302|title=vinayapitaka 302|style=reference}}

## 与释迦族的姻亲

《法句义注》记他与憍萨罗王的关系："*Tena kho pana samayena bimbisāro ca pasenadi kosalo ca aññamaññaṃ bhaginipatikā honti*"（当时频毗娑罗与憍萨罗的波斯匿互为姊妹之夫）。{{para|id=122-1108|title=dhammapada-aṭṭhakathā 1108|style=reference}}

他也是「五大富豪」所在之国的君主："*bimbisārarañño pana vijite pañca amitabhogā nāma ahesuṃ – jotiko, jaṭilo, meṇḍako, puṇṇako, kākavaliyoti*"（频毗娑罗王境内有五位财富无量者：树提、结发者、曼达迦、富那迦、迦迦瓦利耶）。{{para|id=122-1107|title=dhammapada-aṭṭhakathā 1107|style=reference}}
