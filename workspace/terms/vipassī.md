**毗婆尸佛**（Vipassī Buddha），二十四佛之一，在弗沙佛之后出世，是七佛中的第一位。城名槃头婆提（Bandhumatī），父为槃头王，母名槃头婆提；上首弟子为坎陀与帝须，侍者名阿输迦，菩提树为波咤利树。「毗婆尸」一名义为「善观、别别而观」，同形的形容词 *vipassī*「具观者」须与佛名区分。

## 目录

## 出世

《佛种姓经》记："*Phussassa ca aparena, sambuddho dvipaduttamo*"（弗沙之后，有正觉者、两足中的最上者）、"*Vipassī nāma nāmena, loke uppajji cakkhumā*"（名为毗婆尸的具眼者出现于世）。{{para|id=145-2467|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2467|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2468|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2468|style=reference}}

"*Avijjaṃ sabbaṃ padāletvā, patto sambodhimuttamaṃ*"（破尽一切无明，证得无上正觉）、"*Dhammacakkaṃ pavattetuṃ, pakkāmi bandhumatīpuraṃ*"（为转法轮而前往槃头婆提城）。{{para|id=145-2470|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2470|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2471|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2471|style=reference}}

## 名义

《善妙悦意（长部义注）》释此名："*vipassīti tassa nāmaṃ, tañca kho vividhe atthe passanakusalatāya laddhaṃ*"（「毗婆尸」是他的名字；此名得自他善于观见种种义）。{{para|id=104-73|title=sumaṅgalavilāsinī 73|style=reference}}

同书另处更详："*Vipassīti ettha ayaṃ vacanattho, antarantarā nimīlajanitandhakāravirahena visuddhaṃ passati, vivaṭehi ca akkhīhi passatīti vipassī*"（「毗婆尸」的语义如下：不因时时闭目而生黑暗，见得清净，又以张开的眼而见，故名毗婆尸）；"*dutiyavāre viceyya viceyya passatīti vipassī; vicinitvā vicinitvā passatīti attho*"（第二释：别别拣择而见，故名毗婆尸；意即一一简择而见）。{{para|id=104-165|title=sumaṅgalavilāsinī 165|style=reference}}

## 城邑与父母

"*Nagaraṃ bandhumatī nāma, bandhumā nāma khattiyo*"（城名槃头婆提，剎帝利名槃头）、"*Mātā bandhumatī nāma, vipassissa mahesino*"（毗婆尸大仙的母亲名槃头婆提）。{{para|id=145-2539|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2539|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2540|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2540|style=reference}}

"*Aṭṭhavassasahassāni, agāraṃ ajjha so vasi*"（他在家住了八千年）、"*Nando sunando sirimā, tayo pāsādamuttamā*"（难陀、善难陀、尸利摩——三座上妙宫殿）；"*Ticattārīsasahassāni, nāriyo samalaṅkatā*"（四万三千名盛装女子）、"*Sudassanā nāma sā nārī, samavattakkhandho nāma atrajo*"（其妻名善见，其子名平肩）。{{para|id=145-2542|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2542|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2543|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2543|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2545|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2545|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2546|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2546|style=reference}}

## 出家与转法轮

"*Nimitte caturo disvā, rathayānena nikkhami*"（见四种相后乘车出家）；"*Vatti cakkaṃ mahāvīro, migadāye naruttamo*"（人中最上的大雄在鹿野苑转动法轮）。{{para|id=145-2548|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2548|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2552|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2552|style=reference}}

## 弟子与菩提树

"*Khaṇḍo ca tissanāmo ca, ahesuṃ aggasāvakā*"（坎陀与名叫帝须的，是上首弟子）、"*Asoko nāmupaṭṭhāko, vipassissa mahesino*"（毗婆尸大仙的侍者名阿输迦）。{{para|id=145-2554|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2554|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2555|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2555|style=reference}}

"*Candā ca candamittā ca, ahesuṃ aggasāvikā*"（旃陀与旃陀蜜多是上首弟子尼）、"*Bodhi tassa bhagavato, pāṭalīti pavuccati*"（那位世尊的菩提树称为波咤利）。{{para|id=145-2557|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2557|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2558|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2558|style=reference}}

"*Punabbasumitto nāgo ca, ahesuṃ aggupaṭṭhakā*"（富那婆苏蜜多与那伽是上首优婆塞）、"*Sirimā uttarā ceva, ahesuṃ aggupaṭṭhikā*"（尸利摩与郁多罗是上首优婆夷）。{{para|id=145-2560|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2560|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2561|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2561|style=reference}}

## 身量与寿量

"*Asītihatthamubbedho, vipassī lokanāyako*"（世间导师毗婆尸身高八十肘）；"*Asītivassasahassāni, āyu buddhassa tāvade*"（那时佛的寿量是八万年）。{{para|id=145-2563|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2563|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2566|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2566|style=reference}}

## 授记乔达摩

《佛种姓经》记他为菩萨授记，预言未来佛的父母与弟子："*Pitā suddhodano nāma, ayaṃ hessati gotamo*"（父亲名净饭，此人将成为乔达摩）；"*Ānando nāmupaṭṭhāko, upaṭṭhissatimaṃ jinaṃ*"（侍者名阿难，将承事这位胜者）、"*Khemā uppalavaṇṇā ca, aggā hessanti sāvikā*"（差摩与莲华色将是上首弟子尼）。{{para|id=145-2520|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2520|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2524|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2524|style=reference}}{{para|id=145-2526|title=buddhavaṃsapāḷi 2526|style=reference}}

## 同形的形容词

*vipassī* 亦是形容词，义为「具观者、别别观见者」。《譬喻义注》即如此释："*vipassīti visesena, vividhaṃ vā passatīti vipassī*"（「毗婆尸」，即：殊胜地见、或种种地见，故名毗婆尸）。{{para|id=107-3290|title=apadāna-aṭṭhakathā 3290|style=reference}}

因此检索此名时，须依上下文分辨所指是佛名还是形容词。
