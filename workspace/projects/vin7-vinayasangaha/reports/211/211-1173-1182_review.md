## Cross-paragraph consistency

- **pāṭipada**: §1173 (sentences 46-60 and 61-152) and §1176 (sentences 99-131) all render it as 初一. In Chinese, 初一 means the first day of a lunar month, which is the new-moon day. Here pāṭipada is the first day after the full moon, the first day of the dark half of the month (nissaya: ဥပုသ်ကျော်တက် အထွက် ၁-ရက်နေ့, "the first day after the uposatha"). Readers will mistake it for the new moon. → Use 满月后的第一天 ("the first day after the full moon") or 黑半月第一天 ("the first day of the dark half") consistently in all three places.
- **upasampādenti**: §1173 sentence 46-60 has 让十九岁的沙弥……受具足戒 ("let the 19-year-old novice … receive full ordination"). §1173 sentence 61-152 has 为沙弥授具足戒 ("give full ordination to the novice"). → Use 为……授具足戒 consistently, which matches parent §682.
- **How bold explained words are handled**: some are kept in Pāli (**adhimāso** / **adhimāsaṃ** / **na hi**) and others are translated (**第三** / **所以** / **确定无疑**). Within the Pāli-kept group there is also inconsistency: §1173 sentence 2-24 glosses **adhimāso** with (闰月) the first time and not the second time. Keeping Pāli is reasonable when the gloss discusses grammatical gender or particles. But the form should be uniform: give the Pāli bold plus a Chinese gloss in parentheses on first occurrence, and only the Pāli bold afterwards. Whether these words have to match the parent Chinese still needs checking once the correct parent segments are fetched.
- **Names for compound types**: §1173 sentence 2-24 uses 持业释 for kammadhāraya, a term from the traditional six-compound system. §1173 sentence 25-45 uses 不变词复合词 for abyayībhāva, which is a modern grammar label. The two registers don't match. → Use 邻近释 (or 不变释) for the latter, with (abyayībhāva) noted in parentheses on first occurrence.
- **Book and chapter titles across layers**: parent §684 calls the root text 《律藏外抉择摄》, while §1177 here calls it 《律摄》 (Vinayasaṅgaha). Parent §685 writes 入雨安居抉择论 without title marks, while §1178 here wraps the chapter title in 《》. See the sentence-level entries below.

## §1173 sentence 211-1173-25-45
- **Issue**: Terminology. The register of 不变词复合词 doesn't match 持业释 in the previous sentence. The gloss (ghaṭikā) is fine, but the original text has ghaṭiyo (from ghaṭī), so the gloss doesn't correspond to the form in the text.
- **Suggestion**: Change to 邻近释（abyayībhāva）. Change the gloss to (ghaṭī), or drop it.

## §1173 sentence 211-1173-46-60
- **Issue**: Ambiguity / doubtful reading. 出离满月 (nikkhamanīyapuṇṇamāsī) is a literal rendering, and readers can't tell which full moon it is. The nissaya ထွက်သောလပြည့်နေ့ ("the outgoing full moon day") likewise only gives the literal sense. 初一: see Cross-paragraph consistency.
- **Suggestion**: Make clear which full moon nikkhamanīya refers to. If it can't be settled, keep the current rendering, lower the confidence, and record it in the evaluate report. Change 初一 to 满月后的第一天. Change 让……受具足戒 to 为……授具足戒.

## §1175 sentence 211-1175-51-59
- **Issue**: Ambiguous meaning, and the nissaya differs. The translation renders suddhavedikānaṃ as 纯粹的吠陀学者 ("pure Vedic scholars"). The nissaya reads it as သန့်ရှင်းသောအသိဉာဏ်ရှိသည်လည်းဖြစ်သော ရဟန်းတို့ ("monks with pure knowledge"). Note that in §1176 sentence 24-28 the nissaya glosses vedikā as ဗေဒင်ဆရာ (masters of veda / astrology). In this calendrical context, "experts in Vedic calendrical reckoning" fits better than the general 吠陀学者 ("Vedic scholars"). 有些 ("some") is also added by the translator; the original has no such word.
- **Suggestion**: Take the reading that vedika means "expert in calendrical reckoning", for example 单靠历算的人也说 ("even people who rely only on calendrical reckoning say"), and change §1176 sentence 24-28 to match. Delete 有些. Record the disagreement with the nissaya in the report and lower the confidence of this sentence.

## §1176 sentence 211-1176-29-98
- **Issue**: Wording. suddhamāsameva ākaḍḍhitukāmo is rendered 只想推一个整月 ("only wants to push back a whole month"). 整月 is easily misread as "a full month as the amount". The original contrasts with sadivasaṃ māsaṃ ("the month together with its days"). The nissaya သက်သက်သော လ means "just the month".
- **Suggestion**: Change to 只想推月份本身、不连日数 ("only wants to push back the month itself, without the days").
