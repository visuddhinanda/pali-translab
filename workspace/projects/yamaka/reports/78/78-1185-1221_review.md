## Cross-paragraph consistency

1. **How the present tense *nirujjhati* is translated (must fix, across the whole chunk)**: every *na nirujjhati* is rendered as "不灭" (does not cease), and *nirujjhati* as "灭" (ceases) (§1186–1202).
   - **Commentary**: *maraṇakāle nirodhavasena nirodhavāro kathito* (§1687). The cessation section is about the moment of death, and *suddhāvāse parinibbantānaṃ … cuticittassa bhaṅgakkhaṇe ṭhitānaṃ* (§1688) places the cessation at the dissolution moment of the death-consciousness. Following the same pattern, the present tense *uppajjati* is glossed as *uppādakkhaṇasamaṅgitāya* (§1686). So the present tense means "is ceasing right now, at this moment".
   - Leaving "色蕴不灭" (the form aggregate does not cease) without an aspect marker lets Chinese readers take it as "does not perish, is permanent". That is a doctrinal misreading, not a question of style. It is also inconsistent inside the same sentence: the subject already carries the aspect marker ("正在投生者", those being reborn), but the main verb doesn't.
   - **Suggestion**: present tense → "正在灭" (is ceasing) / "不在灭" (is not ceasing); future → "将灭" (will cease) / "将不会灭" (will not cease); past → "已灭" (has ceased), unchanged. Apply this across the whole section.

2. **The added "也" (also) in questions**: in Ka questions §1186, §1190, §1195 and §1199, the second clause adds "也": "他的受蕴也将不灭吗" (will his feeling aggregate also not cease?). §1205, §1208, §1214 and §1218 have no "也". The Pāli has no *pi* in any of them. → **Delete it in all of them**, so that the pattern is "他的受蕴将不会灭吗？" (will his feeling aggregate not cease?). Only the answer sentences with *… ca … ca* keep "也".

3. **Translation of *asaññasatta***: the text has "投生到无想有情" (reborn into the beings without perception) (§1196, §1198-21) and "从无想有情死殁" (passing away from the beings without perception) (§1198-2). *Asaññasatta* names a class of beings, but here it is the place of rebirth. The **commentary** says *asaññasattaṃ upapajjantānanti … asaññasattabhavaṃ upapajjantānaṃ* (§1686), i.e. it refers to that existence. → **Suggest** "投生到无想有情界" (reborn into the realm of beings without perception) and "从无想有情界死殁" (passing away from that realm). When it stands as the subject (*asaññasattānaṃ*, §1200, §1202, §1215), keep "无想有情".

4. **Locatives with *pañcavokāre* / *arūpe***: the text has "正在五蕴有中般涅槃者" (those attaining final nibbāna in the five-aggregate existence, with "中") (§1189, §1198, §1215) but "正在无色界般涅槃者" (those attaining final nibbāna in the formless realm, without "中") (§1187, §1189, §1196, §1198). The Pāli constructions are identical. → Use the same form throughout, e.g. "在五蕴有中…" / "在无色界中…" ("in the …", both with "中").

5. **Whether "的" appears in "他们在那里的X蕴"**: the first half of an answer has "他们在那里色蕴不灭" (no "的"), while the second half and the questions have "他们在那里的受蕴" (with "的", "their … aggregate there") (§1196, §1198, §1200, §1202, §1215, §1217, §1219, §1221). → **Always add "的"**.

6. **Heading terms that don't pair up**: *paccanīka* is rendered "否定式" (negative form) (§1185, §1192, §1194), but *anuloma* is rendered "顺式" (forward form) (§1204, §1211, §1213). "顺" and "否定" are not a matched pair. In Abhidhamma, anuloma/paccanīka are traditionally rendered 顺/逆 (forward/reverse), and the commentary also contrasts *anulomanayo* with *paṭilomanayo* (§1676). → **Suggest** "逆式：人" / "逆式：处" / "逆式：人与处" (reverse form: person / place / person and place). Earlier chunks need the same check, so hand this to harmonize.

7. **Translation of *itaresaṃ***: §1206 and §1209 have "其余的人" (the other people), while §1215, §1217, §1219 and §1221 have "其余…众生" (the other … beings). The Pāli differs (only the latter add *pañcavokārānaṃ* etc.), so it is acceptable. Still, for a uniform register, use "其余的人" when it stands alone, and "其余的五蕴有众生" (the other beings of the five-aggregate existence) and similar when it is qualified.

## §1187 sentence 78-1187-2-17
- **Problem**: double negative and the scope of the negation. In "但他们的受蕴并非将不灭" (but their feeling aggregate is not going-to-not-cease), "并非" sits after the subject, so it reads as describing a property of the feeling aggregate. The Pāli *no ca tesaṃ … na nirujjhissati* negates the whole proposition. Combined with the tense issue, the sentence is easy to misread.
- **Suggestion**: move "并非" in front of the whole clause: "一切正在投生的人和正从无色界死殁的人，他们的色蕴不在灭，但并非他们的受蕴将不会灭" (for all those being reborn and those passing away from the formless realm, their form aggregate is not ceasing, but it is not the case that their feeling aggregate will not cease). The same pattern applies to the *no ca* clauses in §1189, 1196, 1198, 1200, 1202, 1206, 1209, 1215, 1217, 1219 and 1221.

## §1189 sentence 78-1189-2-15
- **Problem**: in "但他们的色蕴并非不灭" (but their form aggregate is not non-ceasing), "并非不灭" combined with "不灭" is very easy to read as "is not imperishable". The intended meaning is that the form aggregate is ceasing at the moment of death (**commentary** §1687, cessation taken at the moment of death).
- **Suggestion**: "但并非他们的色蕴不在灭" (but it is not the case that their form aggregate is not ceasing).

## §1191 sentence 78-1191-16-17

- **Suggestion**: "正在灭" (is ceasing). Leave "将灭" (will cease) in §1190-17 unchanged.

## Overall register

This is not a must-fix, but it is suggested: in the subject position of answer sentences, change them to "…的人" (the people who …), e.g. "正在投生的人" (people being reborn), "正在般涅槃的人" (people attaining final nibbāna). Then "他们" (they) refers to them more naturally, and the result matches `style.md` §1, "宁可白一点" (better a bit more colloquial).
