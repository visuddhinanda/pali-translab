## §1045 sentence 173-1045-2-31
- **Problem:** Mistranslation (number / subject). In "dosacaritattā vā khippaṃ … virāgaṃ uppādesi", *dosacaritattā* refers back to the singular *thero* earlier in the sentence. The translation has "正因为**他们**是瞋行者", wrongly making the subject plural.
- **Fix:** Change to "正因为**他**是瞋行者，所以到第三轮的时候很快就生起了离贪".

## §1045 sentence 173-1045-2-31 (second issue)
- **Problems:**
  - **Subject ambiguity:** In "Thero kira dosacarito ahosi, tasmā … avatvā upāyena kathesi", the elder with the temperament of anger (*dosacarita*) is the one being spoken to. The person who spoke tactfully (*kathesi*) is most likely the visiting monk, not that elder. The current translation, "那位长老据说是瞋行者，所以一开始并没有直接说……而是用方便来开导", reads as if the same elder is speaking.
  - **Form of address:** *tumhe* here is an honorific plural addressed to the one elder, so "你们" is a mistranslation. It should be "您".
- **Fix:** Add the speaker explicitly once 97:2159–2161 confirms who it is (e.g. "所以那位比丘一开始并没有直接对他说'您不是漏尽者'，而是用方便来开导"). Lower this sentence's confidence until the parent passage is checked.

## §1042 sentence 173-1042-2-38
- **Problem:** Wording. Rendering *mānuppādako* (the person in whom conceit arises) as "引起慢的" can be misread as "causing conceit in others".
- **Fix:** Change "引起慢的" to "生起慢的" (it appears twice).

## §1048 sentence 173-1048-2-14
- **Problem:** Lost connective. In "attano amaraṇatthāya devabhāvatthāya vā", *vā* separates two alternatives (or glosses one with the other). The current "为了自己不死、为了成为天神" reads as a single list, so that relationship is lost.
- **Fix:** Change to "为了自己不死，或者说为了成为天神而起的寻".

## §1049 sentence 173-1049-26-33
- **Problem:** Overstated tone. *attho yujjati* means "this meaning is fitting". "这样解释才讲得通" overstates it into "only this explanation works". The ṭīkā is preferring one of the parent's two explanations ("在混杂而住的生活中 / 在投身其中的时候"), not ruling out every other reading.
- **Fix:** Change to "……这样理解是恰当的".
