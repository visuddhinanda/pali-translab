## Cross-paragraph consistency

- **bhindati**: §35 uses “否定” (in line with parent 97-48-12), while §37 uses “打破” throughout. The two contexts mean different things: §35 is about refuting personal ideas of what is pleasing, and §37 is about breaking the near/far distinction. Using different words is reasonable. Harmonize only needs to confirm that parent §50-52 also uses “打破” for bhindamāno.
- **kāmaguṇa**: this layer consistently uses “欲功德” (§30). The parent layer is itself inconsistent: 97-38-27 has “欲境（kāmaguṇa）” while 97-39-7 and 97-41-2 have “欲功德”. Recommend that harmonize changes parent 97-38-27 to “欲功德” so both layers match.
- **“以……等语”** (introducing a quotation): used in §31, §33 and §34, and §35 uses “用……等语”. This is inconsistent across paragraphs and also leans literary (see the style items below). Standardize on “用“……”等话”. Parent 97-38-21 “等语” should be harmonized at the same time.
- **Chapter title**: §38 has “色蕴解说的注释结束”, but the parent chapter title 97-18 is “色蕴的解说”. Recommend “色蕴的解说的注释结束” or “《色蕴的解说》注释结束” so the title matches the parent chapter title.

## §31 sentence 173-31-8-38
- **Issue**: Uncertain reading: *iṭṭhāniṭṭhābhāvato* can be split as iṭṭhāniṭṭha + **abhāva** (“has no distinction between agreeable and disagreeable”) or as iṭṭhāniṭṭha + **bhāva** (“the same object is both agreeable and disagreeable”). The context, which paraphrases the sophist's claim that the same object is agreeable to one person and disagreeable to another, fits the second reading better. The translation takes the first. The meaning comes out about the same, but the wording differs.
- **Suggestion**: Consider “所缘的自性本身既是可喜的又是不可喜的”. Leave this to revise; lower the sentence's confidence.

## §31 sentence 173-31-50-60
- **Issue**: Accuracy: the subject of *dasseti* is the commentary text. “作者表明” is vague and could make readers think it refers to the sub-commentary's author.
- **Suggestion**: Change to “义注表明”, or drop the subject: “……——这表明，“达到悦意极致”这句话……”

## §32 sentence 173-32-16-30
- **Issue**: Style: “其用意” uses the literary pronoun 其. Accuracy: *idaṃ iṭṭhaṃ aniṭṭhañca hoti* means that this already-determined object becomes agreeable or disagreeable to others. “这个可喜、那个不可喜” makes it sound like two different objects.
- **Suggestion**: ……其他人则是由于颠倒，这个所缘对他们来说才会成为可喜的或不可喜的——这就是它的用意。

## §32 sentence 173-32-31-48
- **Issue**: Meaning not fully brought out: the point is that the determined agreeable object, once the faculties are altered by an imbalance of the elements, can **in reverse** become a cause of unwholesome result (and the disagreeable of wholesome result). “它们也会成为善果报或不善果报生起的因” does not show that the result is reversed, so readers cannot see how this sentence connects to the cold-water example that follows.
- **Suggestion**: ……也可以说，由于诸界失调而使根发生变异等，它们还会反过来成为善果报或不善果报生起的因。
